01 FEATURED PROJECT

Automating BOM entry and ERP review.

Replacing work-center-by-work-center manual bill-of-materials entry with a controlled PDF-to-import workflow that prepares validated files for upload and review in Acumatica.

MY ROLE
Workflow design, Python automation and accounting controls
TOOLS

EXAMPLE
Interactive workflow walkthrough

The process before automation

Engineering BOMs were entered manually into the ERP by individual work center, then cross-verified manually. Repeating that entry across work centers created duplicate handling, consumed accounting/operations time, and created opportunities for transposition or omitted lines.

The process I designed

I built and iteratively improved a Python-based automation that reads engineering BOM PDFs, interprets component tables, checks item IDs and quantities, and generates separate import-ready Excel files by work center where the drawing defines that routing. Instead of rekeying all components, the team can upload the generated files through its approved Acumatica process and review the BOM inside the ERP.

Before → after

BEFOREDrawing → manual entry for each work center → manual cross-verification

Individual lines keyed into the system, then checked against source documents.

AFTERDrawing → automated extraction & controls → work-center import files → ERP upload & in-system review

Focus shifts from repeated data entry to exception resolution and verification in the system.

Controls and engineering decisions

  1. Evidence-based table extraction: detects ruled BOM grids across engineering PDF pages and checks that column coverage is complete.
  2. Item integrity: validates part numbers against an ERP inventory master; ambiguous or unverified items trigger explicit review rather than silent replacement.
  3. Quantity reconciliation: checks required values, extensions, skipped rows and continuation pages; keeps legitimately repeated component lines separate.
  4. Work-center-specific output: separates imports by work center where routing information exists; a missing work-center column is handled as a specific documented case rather than silently inferred.
  5. ERP-file compatibility: tests the generated spreadsheet structure and expected import headers, addressing failures caused by Excel file encoding differences or ERP mapping changes.
  6. Safeguards and testing: incorporates review/hold/warning paths, audit logs, preview-oriented checks, and regression tests for previously encountered failure modes.

Why the change matters

The principal benefit is not simply faster file conversion: it removes repeated manual BOM line entry, creates consistent work-center upload batches, and supports review inside the ERP. Quality controls shift earlier in the process, while the accounting and operations teams retain ownership of resolving exceptions and confirming the uploaded result.

The broader AP, AR and BOM automation efforts are collectively estimated to save 5–10 team hours per week, not per tool.
