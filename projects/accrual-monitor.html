CASE STUDY

Purchase Accrual Monitoring & Reconciliation.

I built a report to age open purchase accruals, flag items needing follow-up, and compare the detail with the GL.

FOCUS
Month-end close · Purchasing · Accounts payable
TOOLS / METHODS
JavaScript · ERP report parsing · Age analysis · Account reconciliation
APPLICATION
Private-equity-backed manufacturing finance operations

What needed follow-up

Accrued receipts, vendor bills and returns need to be reviewed together. Unexplained aged items can persist through close cycles unless someone organizes the population and assigns the right follow-up.

What I built

I built a purchase-accrual monitor that can review accrual reports, age underlying documents, classify common scenarios and reconcile supported accrued amounts to the applicable GL/account report controls.

Checks I added

  1. Separate timing differences from items requiring an AP, receiving, or vendor follow-up.
  2. Identify aging risks, partial billings and receipt/return relationships for targeted review.
  3. Compare item-level accrual populations to source report totals and GL values; require explicit investigation of differences.
  4. Support review notes and structured Excel/PDF reporting while retaining human resolution ownership.

How it helps at close

Instead of sorting through the same reports each month, accounting can focus on older receipts, unmatched bills, and balances that don't tie out.
