08 PROJECT CASE STUDY

Customer Credit Exposure & Limit Monitoring.

A decision-ready view of committed sales orders, open receivables, and unapplied credits against an approved credit limit.

FOCUS
Credit risk · AR · Order management
TOOLS / METHODS
Browser-based JavaScript · Spreadsheet parsing · Reconciliation
APPLICATION
Private-equity-backed manufacturing finance operations

The challenge

Customer risk cannot be evaluated reliably from the outstanding AR balance alone. Open orders can create incremental exposure, while unapplied payments, prepayments and credits offset it. A trustworthy report also needs to reconcile back to the ERP.

What I built

I created a tool that reads Acumatica sales order and customer detail exports, organizes eligible open orders and unpaid AR, nets eligible unapplied credits, and compares the combined exposure with a separately confirmed approved credit limit.

Design and control decisions

  1. Use net exposure = qualifying open orders + unpaid invoices − unapplied credits instead of AR alone.
  2. Tie the computed net AR value back to the ERP’s reported balance by documents and identify mismatches.
  3. Display utilization, over-limit amounts and available credit without treating the report as an autonomous lending decision.
  4. Prepare a report that sales and accounting can use to discuss holds and releases using verified, current records.

Business impact

Improves consistency and visibility in customer credit discussions by putting commitments, collections and available credit on one reviewable page.
