CASE STUDY

Customer AP Statement Packager.

I built a way to give customers one clear invoice list and a summary of what's due by location.

FOCUS
Collections · Receivable operations · Reporting
TOOLS / METHODS
JavaScript · Spreadsheet normalization · Aging · Excel workbook generation
APPLICATION
Private-equity-backed manufacturing finance operations

Why statements needed work

Multi-tab statement workbooks and raw customer-detail exports are time-consuming for customers to work through, especially when multiple locations have overdue invoices.

What I built

I built a browser-based statement packaging workflow that accepts customer-detail or formatted statement workbooks, recognizes the source layout, creates a consolidated invoice register and prepares a location-level summary.

Checks and output

  1. Normalize different incoming statement layouts into one invoice-based population.
  2. Allow a clearly marked past-due-only filter and zero-balance exclusion instead of quietly dropping activity.
  3. Compare calculated totals with the source workbook totals and show unresolved variances.
  4. Produce an organized, filterable invoice register and location summary designed for customer AP follow-up.

What changed

The customer gets a clearer breakdown of what's outstanding, and accounting spends less time reformatting the same information.
