CASE STUDY

Company-Wide Pricing & ERP Price Book Management.

When leadership approves pricing changes, I prepare the worksheets and make the corresponding updates in Acumatica.

FOCUS
Commercial finance · Pricing operations · ERP governance
TOOLS / METHODS
Acumatica · Price books · Pricing analysis · Sales worksheets
APPLICATION
Manufacturing accounting and commercial operations

Why this takes careful work

A single company-wide pricing change has multiple downstream points of execution: product prices, sales worksheets, dealer price books and customer-specific price books. Disconnected updates can cause inconsistent quotations or customer pricing.

What I handle

I own the operational implementation of company-wide pricing updates. The CFO develops pricing or requests analysis from me; I prepare supporting analysis and the Acumatica sales worksheets, then update MSRP, dealer pricing and additional customer price books. There are more than three price books to coordinate, together with multiple ERP update points.

What I check

  1. Translate finance leadership's approved pricing direction into the appropriate sales worksheet and ERP updates.
  2. Maintain consistency across MSRP, dealer prices and other customer-specific price books without treating them as a single undifferentiated price.
  3. Coordinate the related ERP records and workflows so sales teams can quote from updated price information.
  4. Bring accounting analysis and Acumatica implementation together to support accurate commercial execution.

Why it matters

Pricing updates have to reach every relevant price book, not just one list. My job is to make sure the approved prices are reflected consistently in the ERP so the sales team has the right information.
