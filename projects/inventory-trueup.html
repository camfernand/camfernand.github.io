CASE STUDY

Inventory Valuation & GL True-Up.

I built a tool to compare inventory valuation reports to GL balances, flag differences, and prepare adjustments for review.

FOCUS
Cost accounting · Inventory valuation · Financial close
TOOLS / METHODS
JavaScript · ERP exports · Inventory/GL mapping · Reconciliation
APPLICATION
Private-equity-backed manufacturing finance operations

The problem I wanted to solve

Inventory valuation reports and balance sheet accounts can diverge through mapping, timing or in-transit activity. Blindly posting a balancing journal entry risks moving real inventory into cost of goods sold incorrectly.

What I built

I created a browser-based inventory true-up tool that joins valuation detail with stock-item classifications, reconciles in-scope accounts to GL balances, surfaces unmatched items and builds a balance-sheet coverage bridge.

Checks I built in

  1. Link inventory-item detail to the applicable account without hiding missing or out-of-scope classifications.
  2. Separate in-transit inventory and flag inconsistencies between posted account and warehouse indicators.
  3. Produce proposed journal-entry adjustments only from visible differences requiring approval.
  4. Display valuation-to-GL and balance-sheet tie-outs, with explicit warnings when controls do not reconcile.
  5. Preserve preparer/reviewer sign-off rather than automatically posting entries.

Why it's useful

The tool points out differences instead of just making the totals match. That gives accounting a chance to review timing and mapping issues before recommending an adjustment.
