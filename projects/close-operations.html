06 PROJECT CASE STUDY

Faster close & scalable processes.

Helping improve accounting cadence while strengthening the ERP workflows and cross-functional handoffs required by a growing business.

FOCUS
Month-end close, process redesign
TOOLS / DISCIPLINES
Acumatica · Excel · Reconciliations · Internal controls
PERIOD
2024–2026

The operational challenge

As an accounting environment grows, inconsistent source data, unclear ownership, and repeated manual work can extend the close cycle and divert time away from meaningful review.

My contribution

I have owned substantial parts of month-end close, including accruals, journal entries, reconciliations, fixed assets, prepaids, inventory-related reporting, and support for AP/AR. I also contributed to integration and process scaling for acquired operations in a private-equity-backed environment.

What changed

I contributed to a team-wide improvement in month-end close cadence, from more than ten days to approximately five, through process standardization, Acumatica improvements and clearer handoffs. I also supported workflows including Acumatica import scenarios, Generic Inquiries, and purchase order approval process design.

Principles behind the improvement

  1. Identify upstream dependencies and late data inputs.
  2. Standardize recurring schedules and checks.
  3. Automate repeatable preparation steps without skipping review.
  4. Assign clear owners for exceptions and approvals.
  5. Make reporting more consistent across close periods.

Why it matters

A dependable close supports timelier reporting, better executive visibility, and more capacity for analysis and improvement after the books are finalized.
