CASE STUDY

Month-end close process efficiency.

I've improved month-end close tasks by documenting procedures and reducing manual preparation, while keeping reviews and reconciliations in place.

FOCUS
Month-end close, process redesign
TOOLS / DISCIPLINES
Acumatica · Excel · Reconciliations · Internal controls
PERIOD
2024–2026

What was slowing us down

Much of the close involved repeated exports, manual preparation, and follow-ups. That took time away from reviewing reconciliations and investigating exceptions.

What I handle

I have owned substantial parts of month-end close, including accruals, journal entries, reconciliations, fixed assets, prepaids, inventory-related reporting, and support for AP/AR. I also contributed to integration and process scaling for acquired operations in a private-equity-backed environment.

What I changed

I documented recurring close steps and improved how we use Acumatica exports, import scenarios, and Generic Inquiries. I also built processes that reduce repetitive preparation and make it easier to review reconciliations, accruals, and exceptions.

What I focused on

  1. Identify upstream dependencies and late data inputs.
  2. Standardize recurring schedules and checks.
  3. Automate repeatable preparation steps without skipping review.
  4. Assign clear owners for exceptions and approvals.
  5. Make reporting more consistent across close periods.

The result

These changes reduced the amount of repetitive work involved in individual close tasks. The overall close date depends on many factors, but the processes I improved are more consistent and easier to repeat.

SELECTED TAKEAWAY
Less manual work, more repeatable controls

Less repeated preparation, clearer steps, and more consistent reconciliation checks.
