CASE STUDY

Product-line margin analysis.

I prepare product-line margin reports for our CEO and CFO, with a focus on understanding costs and profitability.

FOCUS
Financial analysis, executive reporting
TOOLS / DISCIPLINES
Acumatica Generic Inquiries · Excel · Cost accounting
PERIOD
2024–2026

What leadership wanted to understand

When leadership asks how a product line is performing, it helps to separate changes in costs from changes in selling prices. That starts with checking the underlying item costs and sales information.

My part of the work

I prepared product-line margin analyses directly for the CEO and CFO and supported inventory valuation, item costing, pricing uploads, and related reporting. I also worked with engineering on BOM structures that influence product cost accuracy.

How I prepare the analysis

  1. Establish the relevant product and reporting population.
  2. Confirm cost definitions, time period, and units of measure.
  3. Reconcile or investigate anomalous cost and margin results.
  4. Group results into actionable product-line views.
  5. Show management the assumptions and unusual items that need context.

How it helps

The reports help leadership see where margins are changing and which items deserve a closer look. My work with inventory and BOMs also helps me investigate unusual costs before presenting the results.

SELECTED TAKEAWAY
Product-line analysis delivered to CEO and CFO

Product margin reports that bring together item costs, sales results, and the questions management is asking.
