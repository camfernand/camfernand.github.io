07 PROJECT CASE STUDY

Client onboarding at scale.

Building a foundation in controls, documentation, customer service, and operational execution in banking.

FOCUS
Client Services Representative II · New Accounts / CSR III · Senior New Accounts Representative
TOOLS / DISCIPLINES
Client onboarding · Treasury support · Training · Compliance
PERIOD
December 2017 – May 2022 · progressive roles

The role

At Poppy Bank, I progressed from client services into new-account responsibilities. I served as Client Services Representative II from December 2017 through May 2021, then progressed through New Accounts Representative / CSR III and Senior New Accounts Representative responsibilities during my May 2021–May 2022 new-accounts tenure. The work covered business and personal accounts, complex entity documentation, treasury-related support, referrals, and client communication.

Selected accomplishments

I handled approximately 50–70 account openings per month in the New Accounts role, supported quarterly business development goals, and contributed to process improvements for Treasury and Business Lending onboarding.

Beyond account openings

I helped train and support staff across multiple branches, maintained accuracy in documentation, and worked across compliance and operations when processes needed improvement.

The carryover into accounting

Banking taught me to handle high-volume processes carefully, recognize the importance of controls, and communicate clearly across teams. Those habits now shape how I approach reporting and accounting automation.
