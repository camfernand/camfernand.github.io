15 PROJECT CASE STUDY

Acquired Product Line Setup & Sales Enablement.

Building the item, BOM and transaction workflow foundations needed for sales teams to quote and deliver an acquired product line.

FOCUS
ERP integration · Product master data · Cross-functional enablement
TOOLS / METHODS
Acumatica · Inventory item naming · BOM coordination · Sales training
APPLICATION
Manufacturing accounting and commercial operations

The challenge

Adding an acquired product line requires more than creating SKUs. Sales needs consistent item records, production needs coordinated BOMs, and quoting, sales-order and shipment documentation may follow a separate workflow from the existing business.

My ownership

Following the acquisition, I designed the product-line inventory naming structure, coordinated with production on BOM creation, and developed template structures for quotes, sales orders and shipments that are handled separately from the usual process. I also organized meetings and taught sales teams how to quote the newly created items and navigate their ERP workflow.

Execution and control design

  1. Create a consistent inventory-item naming structure for the acquired product line.
  2. Coordinate with production so new ERP items align with the BOMs required to manufacture or fulfill them.
  3. Build separate quote, sales-order and shipment templates where the acquired products require a different operational path.
  4. Organize cross-functional sales training to explain the new items, demonstrate quoting, and support adoption.

Business impact

Helped translate an acquisition into operational ERP structures and a usable workflow for sales and production; demonstrates cross-functional implementation and change-management ownership.
