09 PROJECT CASE STUDY

Payment-Behavior Credit Review Analyzer.

Transforming invoice-level payment records into explainable customer risk indicators, with data-quality exceptions kept visible.

FOCUS
Credit risk · Collections · Customer analytics
TOOLS / METHODS
JavaScript · Acumatica GI export · Weighted analysis · Excel/PDF reporting
APPLICATION
Private-equity-backed manufacturing finance operations

The challenge

A single aging snapshot does not describe how customers historically pay. Payment application datasets can also duplicate invoice rows through report joins, and abnormal due dates may distort scores.

What I built

I developed a credit-review workflow that reads paid-invoice applications from an ERP export, removes known join-fanout duplicates, preserves genuine partial applications and calculates customer payment patterns and risk tiers.

Design and control decisions

  1. Calculate amount-weighted days late, simple days late, late-dollar share and other payment-behavior metrics.
  2. Apply documented LOW, MODERATE, ELEVATED and HIGH thresholds to the weighted lateness score.
  3. Flag source anomalies and avoid silently treating duplicated invoice joins as genuine activity.
  4. Offer explicit controls for early-payment flooring, potential re-terming and parent-account consolidation, with settings documented on exports.
  5. Provide summary, detail, exception and methodology views for review rather than automating credit decisions.

Business impact

Gives finance and sales teams a repeatable, explainable starting point for customer credit reviews, with traceable methodology and exceptions.
