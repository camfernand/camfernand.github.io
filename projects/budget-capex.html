Annual budget deck & CapEx analyses.

What the budget work involved

The annual budget needed support from accounting records, especially fixed assets and capital spending. My role was to prepare and check the analysis behind those figures.

What I prepared

I contributed to the annual budget deck and prepared fixed-asset and CapEx analyses for the planning process. My regular responsibilities also include fixed asset and depreciation work, prepaids, and related period-end reporting.

How I approached it

  1. Gather relevant historical records and current asset schedules.
  2. Organize assumptions and supporting detail for review.
  3. Analyze expected depreciation and capital-related considerations.
  4. Prepare reporting that makes variances and key drivers easier to discuss.
  5. Reconcile planning outputs back to known accounting records where applicable.

Why I value this work

I enjoy this type of analysis because it connects the accounting details I work with each month to leadership's decisions about future spending.
