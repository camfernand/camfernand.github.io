05 PROJECT CASE STUDY

Annual budget deck & CapEx analyses.

Supporting annual financial planning through budget deck preparation and fixed-asset/CapEx analyses.

FOCUS
Annual budgeting, CapEx and fixed-asset analysis
TOOLS / DISCIPLINES
Excel · Budget preparation · Fixed assets
PERIOD
2026

The planning need

Budget preparation requires translating detailed general ledger and asset information into reasonable assumptions, reviewable schedules, and concise executive materials.

My contribution

I contributed to the annual budget deck and prepared fixed-asset and CapEx analyses for the planning process. My regular responsibilities also include fixed asset and depreciation work, prepaids, and related period-end reporting.

The analytical approach

  1. Gather relevant historical records and current asset schedules.
  2. Organize assumptions and supporting detail for review.
  3. Analyze expected depreciation and capital-related considerations.
  4. Prepare reporting that makes variances and key drivers easier to discuss.
  5. Reconcile planning outputs back to known accounting records where applicable.

Why it matters

Forward-looking planning is stronger when finance teams can link the story in the presentation to disciplined accounting detail. My involvement bridges operational close work and management-facing planning.
