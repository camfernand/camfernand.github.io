03 PROJECT CASE STUDY

AP & AR process automation.

Simplifying repeatable accounting tasks so the team can focus on review, controls, exceptions, and timely customer/vendor follow-up.

FOCUS
Process improvement, AR/AP oversight
TOOLS / DISCIPLINES
Acumatica · Power Automate Desktop · Excel
PERIOD
2024–2026

The operational problem

Routine accounting work often involves exporting data, reviewing exceptions, preparing entries or imports, and passing documents between systems or team members. Each manual touch point increases processing time and inconsistency.

My contribution

I built and improved AP- and AR-related automation workflows and helped design clearer processes for junior accounting staff. In parallel, I oversaw AR/AP activities, collections-related reporting, and handoffs within month-end operations.

The process design

  1. Identify repeatable steps and required controls.
  2. Separate routine data preparation from judgment-based review.
  3. Standardize templates, file naming, and checks where appropriate.
  4. Provide clear exception paths instead of silently processing bad data.
  5. Document the steps so other staff can use and maintain the workflow.

Impact

Across the AP, AR, and BOM automations, I estimate the department recaptured approximately 1–2 staff-hours per workday, or 5–10 hours in a five-day week. This estimate covers the combined AP, AR and BOM workflows, not each individual automation.
