CASE STUDY

Daily executive cash reporting.

Each morning, I prepare a cash report for leadership using ERP exports and supporting schedules.

FOCUS
Cash visibility, liquidity reporting, financial controls
TOOLS / DISCIPLINES
ERP exports · Excel reporting · AP/AR analysis
CADENCE
Daily executive reporting

Why I built it

Leadership needed one place to review cash and the payments and receipts coming up. The ERP reports contained the details, but they had to be brought together and reviewed.

What I do each morning

I built an Excel report that brings ERP exports into one daily package. Each morning I update the report, review the supporting balances and outstanding items, and send the finished package to executive management.

What the report covers

  1. Brings together cash balances, cash-per-books reporting and outstanding-check activity.
  2. Summarizes accounts receivable, deposits in transit and anticipated incoming cash.
  3. Organizes AP funding requirements, payment timing and other estimated outflows.
  4. Uses supporting schedules and reconciliation checks to flag items requiring review.
  5. Packages reviewed results into a consistent format for executive management.

How it's used

Instead of working through several exports, management can see cash balances, expected receipts, and upcoming payments in one place. The supporting schedules make it easier to trace questions back to the source reports.

Confidentiality: This page describes the process, not the original report. No company balances, account information, customer details, or ERP exports are shared.

SELECTED TAKEAWAY
One daily report for management

Cash positions, incoming receipts, upcoming payments, and supporting reconciliations in one package.
