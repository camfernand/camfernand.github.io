07 PROJECT CASE STUDY

Daily executive cash reporting.

Turning ERP exports into a structured morning cash and liquidity package for executive management.

FOCUS
Cash visibility, liquidity reporting, financial controls
TOOLS / DISCIPLINES
ERP exports · Excel reporting · AP/AR analysis
CADENCE
Daily executive reporting

The reporting need

Bank balances alone do not show available cash or upcoming funding requirements. Executive leadership needs a consistent view of cash positions, expected receipts, outstanding disbursements, and near-term obligations.

My contribution

I developed and maintain a detailed daily cash reporting workbook using exports from the ERP. It consolidates financial information into a standardized executive summary with supporting schedules that I review and package for distribution each morning.

How the package works

  1. Brings together cash balances, cash-per-books reporting and outstanding-check activity.
  2. Summarizes accounts receivable, deposits in transit and anticipated incoming cash.
  3. Organizes AP funding requirements, payment timing and other estimated outflows.
  4. Uses supporting schedules and reconciliation checks to flag items requiring review.
  5. Packages reviewed results into a consistent format for executive management.

Why it matters

The process makes daily liquidity information more accessible and repeatable. It connects accounting detail to executive cash planning while retaining review steps for assumptions and source data.

Confidentiality: This generalized case study does not disclose the employer's workbook, financial amounts, bank identifiers, customer or vendor information, or underlying exports. Inputs and exceptions are subject to manual review; the reporting package is not presented as an unattended live-bank integration.

SELECTED TAKEAWAY
Structured daily cash visibility

A repeatable executive cash reporting package based on ERP exports and supporting controls.
